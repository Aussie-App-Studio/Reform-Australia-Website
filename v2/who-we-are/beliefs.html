What we believe

Most of what we believe we hold in common with the Christian church around the world and throughout the ages. We believe that the Bible is God's inspired Word.

Our understanding of key Christian doctrines is summarised in the three ecumenical creeds (Apostles', Nicene, Athanasian) and in the Reformed Confessions: the Belgic Confession, the Heidelberg Catechism and the Canons of Dort. We also hold the Westminster Confession in high regard and greatly appreciate the "Contemporary Testimony" document produced by the Christian Reformed Church in North America.

For office bearers

Ministers, elders and deacons sign the Form of Subscription, declaring that the teaching of these confessions agrees with the Word of God and promising to teach and defend it.

All of these documents, with the liturgical forms and the Church Order, are in the Forms and documents library.

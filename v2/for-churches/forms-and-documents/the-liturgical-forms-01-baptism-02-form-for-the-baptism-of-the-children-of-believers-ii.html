Form for the Baptism of the Children of Believers (II)

Note: With some modification, this Form could also be

used for Adult Baptism.

The minister shall say:

Beloved brothers and sisters,

Our risen Lord told His apostles, "...go and make disciples of all nations, baptising them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you." According to these words, there is a close connection between the proclamation of His kingdom and baptism. Those who accept the gospel with a believing heart receive in baptism the assurance that their sinful lives are cleansed by the Saviour's blood and are regenerated by the Holy Spirit. This is illustrated by the water used in baptism: for just as water cleanses our body, so the blood of Christ cleanses us from all sins.

The Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit give promises and assurances in baptism. God the Father adopts us as His children and heirs. God the Son covers our sins with His perfect sacrifice. God the Holy Spirit dwells and works in us, recreating us into living members of Christ's body more and more until the great Day, when we shall at last be presented before God in the full beauty of Christ's spotless life.

Baptism is administered only once, but remains valid throughout our life. Through the promises that our Lord makes to us in our baptism, He will enable us to grow in humility, so that He may become greater and we become less. In the covenant with the Lord it is not only He who makes promises, it is also we who have obligations. We are called to a new obedience. Our Lord both demands and encourages His covenant partners to serve Him, to seek His kingdom first, and to forsake the works of darkness. Even when we fall into sin through weakness, we should not despair, nor doubt His grace, but repent and return to our Father.

Scripture calls Abraham the father of all believers. He was given the covenant sign of circumcision after he came to believe in God. However, God also commanded Abraham that this same sign of His grace be given to his small children. The grace of God is still the same today and includes our children in His covenant people. Scripture calls the children of believers holy and God's promises are for them as well as for us. God does not only call individuals but a people to be His very own. It is His will that all nations are to become His disciples.

As brothers and sisters in Christ, we all — and Names of Parents in particular — must not hinder the children from coming to Jesus but must lead them to conversion and encourage them, instruct them and set them an example of Christian living.

Prayer

Dear Father, be merciful to Full Name. May Your Holy Spirit unite him/her with Jesus Christ so that all the benefits of Christ's death and resurrection be his/hers. We rejoice with believing hearts as we see Your love again displayed in the baptism of this child. In the name of Jesus Christ our Saviour. Amen.

The minister requests the parents to stand and says, addressing them by name:

Names of Parents, since you have presented Name of Children for holy baptism, you are asked to answer the following questions before God and His people:

  1. Do you confess Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour? Do you accept the promises of God, and affirm the truth of the Christian faith which is taught in the Bible and confessed in this church of Christ?
  1. Do you believe that your child, though sinful by nature, is received by God in Christ as a member of His covenant and His church, and therefore ought to be baptised?
  1. Do you promise, in reliance on the Holy Spirit and with the help of the Christian community, to do all in your power to instruct this child in the truth of the Christian faith and to lead him/her by your example to confess and follow Jesus Christ as his/her own Lord and Saviour?

Answer:

By the grace of God, we do.

In administering baptism, the minister shall say:

Full Name, I baptise you into the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

The minister invites the congregation to stand, and addresses them as follows:

Do you, the people of the Lord, promise to receive Full Name in love, pray for him/her and help care for his/her instruction in the faith? Do you promise so to order your life and witness as a congregation that he/she may grow up in the knowledge and love of God, and be surrounded by Christian example and influence?

Answer: By the grace of God, we do.

A psalm or hymn is sung.

The minister leads in the baptismal prayer:

Lord our God, forever faithful to Your promise, we thank You for assuring us and our children again that You have forgiven us and received us as children in Christ.

Grant wisdom and love to Names of Parents and to us all as we carry out the vows just made. We pray that You will guide our children throughout their lives and enable them to respond in faith to the gospel. Fill them with Your Spirit and make their lives fruitful. Uphold them in temptation, struggle and pain, and, when Jesus returns, let them celebrate with all the people of God Your greatness and goodness forever, in the joy of Your new creation.

For Jesus' sake. Amen.

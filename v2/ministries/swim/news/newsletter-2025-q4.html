Solomon Islands SWIM news · 31 December 2025

Newsletter: October to December 2025

We give thanks to God for his provision throughout 2025, and appreciate our SIW and LLT leaders for their leadership in another year of mission and Christian work in our communities and the Solomon Islands.

New staff: we welcome Eunice Menapi, our new administration assistant and PR worker. Eunice is married and comes from Temotu.

Testimonies: Margret from the women's ministry thanked God for Tuesdays at SWIM, where she learns the word of God and sewing skills to share with her community. Winnie from the youth ministry said her parents noticed her changed attitude after lessons on building godly character, such as forgiveness. Bobby Kala of Kwainafala said: "For more than 50 years our community Kwainafala have struggled with no water for our daily basic necessities. We thank God that through the SWIM water project that has provided us with building a dam, 2 water tanks and 25 standpipes, we can now have access to clean water." Pastor George from Fouia, East Malaita, said the training on end-time prophecies had blessed leaders serving in remote communities.

Leadership training: more than 70 pastors, women and youth leaders completed a three-day course on end-time prophecies at Baurara, East Fataleka, from 21 to 23 November. The sessions were held in the open air. At Kwainafala, more than 30 church leaders took part in a three-day course from 13 to 15 September, alongside the water project.

Kwainafala water project: 30 households in North Malaita now benefit from a SWIM water project. The community had faced great difficulty getting water, and they see the finished project as an answer to prayer.

Ministries: all the ministries ended the year with celebrations. About 20 women enjoyed songs, dancing and a secret sister gift exchange. Around 40 children shared their memory verses in song and action and received lunch boxes. The sports ministry finished with its championship final, attended by 50 young men. The youth ministry ended with Bible quizzes and water bottles as Christmas gifts.

New solar system: we praise God for a new solar system at the base, which reduces our use of fossil fuel.

Crossroads: more than 50 inmates graduated with certificates in "Great truths of the Bible" in October and "Bible survey" in November. Photos are not available for security reasons.

Praise points: God's goodness and faithfulness in 2025, his provision and blessing, the many lives blessed through SWIM, and the teamwork and unity of the year.

Prayer points: God's direction and provision for SWIM in 2026; wisdom, strength and courage for the SIW and LLT; the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia; SWIM's plans, training and ministries for 2026; more open doors to reach the Solomon Islands with the gospel; and the health and needs of SWIM staff and their families.

Christmas greetings came from Pastor Michael Light and from base managers Joh and Karin Van Straten. "And do not forget to do good and to share with others, for with such sacrifices God is pleased." (Hebrews 13:16)

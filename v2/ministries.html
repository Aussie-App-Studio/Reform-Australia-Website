Ministries

The work CRCA churches do together: with children and young people, in training and church planting, and in mission at home and overseas.

Next generation

  • CadetsA club program run by volunteers in local churches for primary school boys aged 9 to 14.
  • GEMSChristian girls' clubs for girls aged 9 to 15, run by women volunteers from CRCA churches, with Busy Bees for girls aged 6 to 8.
  • Youth ConventionA week in God's Word, worship and community for CRCA young adults aged 16 to 28. Convention 2027 is sold out, with a waitlist open.

Training and church planting

  • Reformed Theological CollegeThe theological college where the CRCA encourages candidates for ordination to study.
  • CandidacyHow to become a candidate for ordained ministry in the CRCA, from first contact through study, vicariate and ordination.
  • Church Planting TaskforceThe Church Planting Taskforce (CPT) encourages CRCA churches in the task of planting churches throughout Australia.
  • Shift CoachingA set of coaching questions to help CRCA pastors and church councils grow in love, discipleship, leadership and outreach.
  • Local Church Outreach TrainingOrganic Outreach training to help every church member share their faith naturally, offered through a partnership with Organic Outreach International.
  • Missional ExplorationsSeed funding from the Church Planting Taskforce for local outreach that could one day grow into a church plant.

Mission and partnerships

  • World TransformThe CRCA's ministry of mercy overseas: showing mercy in Christ's name through development, relief and gospel ministry with partners in India, the Solomon Islands and Papua New Guinea.
  • Solomon Islands SWIMSWIM (Supporting Work in Mission) is the CRCA's mission base in Honiara, Solomon Islands. It offers Bible teaching, leadership training, weekly community ministries, prison ministry and practical support, and hosts short-term mission teams.
  • Other ministries we supportOther ministries we supportChristian organisations outside the CRCA that our churches support through recommended offerings.
  • Ecumenical RelationsA synodical committee that manages the CRCA's relationships with Reformed and Presbyterian churches in Australia and around the world.

Serving the churches

  • Safe Church UnitHelps churches understand and prevent abuse, and respond with compassion and justice where abuse has occurred.
  • Reading sermonsThe Word of Salvation archive of sermons by CRCA ministers, for reading services and study.

Solomon Islands SWIM news · 31 March 2026

Newsletter: January to March 2026

We give thanks to almighty God for his guidance, protection and provision through the 2025 Christmas holidays. As we enter 2026, we continue to pray for God's guidance and protection over our staff and their daily work. We appreciate the SIW and LLT leaders for their continuing leadership and support, and look forward to working together in mission and in supporting Christian work in our communities and the Solomon Islands.

New staff: we welcome our new security officer, Chris Talo, from Guadalcanal Province. Chris also plays in the SWIM FC team.

Visitors: two board members from Australia, Michael and Daniel, visited the base from 28 January to 4 February. Pastor Michael encouraged the staff in their devotion on Joshua 3:3-17, with the theme "taking a leap of faith".

Weekly devotion: the weekly staff devotion helps staff grow in their spiritual life. Pastor Michael Light spoke on Philippians 4:13, "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me", and on John 15:5, "I am the vine; you are the branches."

Biblical leadership training: a three-day course was held from 22 to 25 January at Capernaum Centre, East Mbaelelea, North Malaita, for more than 60 leaders. A two-day course at Bethesda in Honiara ended with more than 20 leaders receiving a certificate of attainment.

SWIM ministries: the sports ministry began the year with devotion and training. Hellen shared from Psalm 119:11 with the women's ministry, which started the year with devotion and activity planning with base manager Karin.

Crossroads prison ministry: the ministry has grown into the provinces. We trained 12 volunteers to teach Crossroads Bible study lessons at Auki Correctional Centre. On Monday 2 February, more than 30 inmates at Tetere Correctional Centre graduated from "Great truths of the Bible" and "Survey of the Bible". On 23 December 2025 a SWIM team provided a Christmas meal at Rove Correctional Centre.

Bookings: guests this quarter included the West AreAre Rokotanikeni Association (WARA) for its first management retreat of 2026, HOPE Trust for its planning day (they thanked us for the food and the workshop setting), Live and Learn for five days of training for rangers from four communities in Marau, and students from Global Mission Bible Institute for a day retreat.

Base work: staff weeded the vegetable garden, which saves buying vegetables at the market, installed new clotheslines, repainted House 8, cleared dry branches, laid gravel on the base road and kept the grounds and Transit House clean. We thank God for new solar lighting, which helps security and reduces fuel costs. We also wished Karin a happy 65th birthday.

Praise points: God's provision, faithfulness and goodness in 2026; financial support from the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia; SWIM's ministries as a helping hand to the surrounding community; and open doors for Crossroads at Tetere and Auki.

Prayer points: SWIM's operation plan for 2026; SWIM staff and families; new ministry opportunities, including feeding the hungry at the dump site; and our partners in the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia.

With grateful hearts we thank every SWIM staff member for their contribution over the past three months.

What Cadets is

The Christian Cadet Corps is an independent youth ministry. Its purpose is to give local churches a ministry program that helps them share Christ's love with boys from their church and community.

Cadets is run by volunteers called counsellors. The program is made for primary school boys aged 9 to 14.

What happens at Cadets

At Cadets the boys learn about God, work on badges, make crafts, play games, sing songs and make new friends. Clubs also go on camps and outings.

Our history

The Cadet movement was founded in the USA in 1952 and came to Australia in 1964.

Since then it has grown to 15 clubs around Australia, with about 180 cadets and 70 counsellors.

What we believe

The basis of the Christian Cadet Corps is the Word of God (the Bible) according to the concepts expressed in the Heidelberg Catechism, the Canons of Dort and the Belgic Confession.

Our goal

Luke 2:52 says that "Jesus grew in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and men."

The goal of the Christian Cadet Corps, based on that verse, is "to help boys grow spiritually in all areas of life (devotional, mental, physical and social) by providing Christian men with a uniquely designed structure, program and materials."

Club theme: Amazing

The club theme is "Amazing", based on Joshua 3:5: "Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the Lord will do amazing things among you." Cadets and GEMS clubs share this theme.

Through the year the boys are reminded:

  • That God is holy
  • That we must prepare ourselves if we are to be in his presence
  • That God has done, is doing and will do amazing things

For counsellors

These reminders help the boys strive to be holy and to recognise the many ways God blesses them.

Counsellors are challenged to be an example to their club: look for, find and share the many amazing things God is doing. Show your boys that God is amazing as you encourage them to live for Jesus.

The theme lessons, bulletin cover and Gold Sheet for the theme are in the resources below.

Finding a club

Cadets clubs meet in local Christian Reformed churches. To find out whether there is a club near you, or to ask about starting one or becoming a counsellor, send us an enquiry or contact your nearest CRCA church.

Form For The Preparation Of The Lord's Supper (I)

The minister or elder leading the service says:

As we prepare to celebrate the Lord's Supper, let us remember that Scripture calls us to examine ourselves before God. Although there is no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus, it is possible to eat and drink a judgment on ourselves if we approach His holy table without realising properly what we are to do there.

Scripture says that, in this sacrament, "we proclaim the death of the Lord until He comes." In sorrow and repentance we are to see how our sins caused Jesus' death, in thankfulness how He fully saves us by His death, in joyful service how we are to respond to our Saviour's death, and in love how we can help others to find peace in that death.

The responses between [ ] may be omitted, or said by the leader of the service, or said by all. In the latter case the leader of the service says:

[Let us now say together:

Show us Your ways, O LORD,

teach us Your paths;

guide us in Your truth and teach us,

for You are God our Saviour,

and our hope is in You, all day long.]

Who are we to proclaim the death of Christ? We, whose sins are the cause of it! For our transgressions He was pierced, and for our iniquities He was crushed. Only in utter humility can we properly celebrate the Supper of our Lord, knowing that "God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble."

[Out of the depths we cry to You, O LORD,

O LORD, hear our voice.

Let Your ears be attentive to our cry for mercy.

If You, O LORD, kept a record of sins,

O LORD, who could stand? ]

The marvellous news of the gospel comes to those who humble themselves before God: When we were still helpless, Christ died for the ungodly. This is how God showed His love among us, He sent His one and only Son into the world that we might live through Him. This is love: not that we loved God but that He loved us and sent His Son as an atoning sacrifice for our sins. God made Him who had no sin to be sin for us, so that in Him — and in Him alone! — we might become the righteousness of God.

[May we never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ,

through which the world has been crucified to us

and we to the world.

Therefore, since we have been justified through faith,

we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. ]

The only possible response to this good news is grateful repentance. Jesus who died our death invites us to live His life in the power of the Holy Spirit. This Lord who makes an everlasting covenant with us calls us to be on His side as we fight sin — in us and around us — with tears and with joy, in gratitude and in hope. It is for this new life in Christ Jesus that He strengthens us by this food and drink at His table.

[How can we repay the LORD for all His goodness to us?

We will lift up the cup of salvation

and call upon the name of the LORD.

We will fulfil our vows to the LORD

in the presence of all His people.]

At His table we do not only meet our Lord; we also meet one another there. As our Lord welcomes us, so we are to welcome our brothers and sisters, struggling saints as we are, and we are to do all we can to encourage them in love.

Therefore, if you know of anything in your life that could be a hindrance to a brother or sister of yours to come to communion with you, you are urged to do all you can to remove such stumbling blocks.

[For none of us lives to himself alone

and none of us dies to himself alone.

If we live, we live to the Lord,

and if we die, we die to the Lord.

So, whether we live or die,

we belong to the Lord. ]

The table of the Lord is for those who humbly trust in Him and seek to follow Him in repentance and obedience. Those, however, who live in self-righteousness, who place their hope in works or virtues of their own, and who do not show love to God and neighbour, have no place at the Lord's Supper unless they repent.

Yet we should not be deterred by any sin and weakness which lingers within us against our will. The God of grace and mercy calls us to turn to Him and live!

Let us pray:

Almighty God,

to whom all hearts are open, all desires known,

and from whom no secrets are hidden:

cleanse the thoughts of our hearts by the guidance

of Your Holy Spirit,

that we may perfectly love You,

and worthily magnify Your holy name,

through Christ our Lord.

[All] Amen.

Form For The Ordination / Installation Of Elders And Deacons

The Opening of the Service

Minister

Praise, O servants of the LORD, praise the name of the LORD!

All

Let the name of the LORD be praised, both now and for evermore.

Minister

From the rising of the sun to the place where it sets,

All

the name of the LORD is to be praised!

Minister

The LORD is exalted over all the nations,

All

His glory above the heavens.

Minister

Who is like the LORD our God,

the One who sits enthroned on high,

who stoops down to look on the heavens and the earth?

He raises the poor from the dust

and lifts the needy from the ash heap;

All

Praise the LORD! Hallelujah!

Minister

Our help is in the name of the LORD

All

Who made heaven and earth!

Minister

Who faithfully keeps His Word

All

and never forsakes the works of His hands!

Minister

Grace unto you and peace

from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ

in the fellowship of the Holy Spirit.

All

Amen!

The congregation sings (Psalm 84, 122, or another suitable psalm or hymn).

Confession and Assurance

Minister

"This is the message we have heard from Him and declare to you:

God is light; in Him there is no darkness at all.

All

If we claim to have fellowship with Him

yet walk in the darkness,

we lie and do not live by the truth.

Minister

But if we walk in the light, as He is in the light,

we have fellowship with one another,

and the blood of Jesus, His Son, purifies us from all sin.

If we claim to be without sin,

we deceive ourselves and the truth is not in us.

All

If we confess our sins,

He is faithful and just

and will forgive us our sins

and purify us from all unrighteousness."

The congregation sings a psalm or hymn expressing confession of sin.

Minister

"Dear friends, let us love one another,

for love comes from God...

This is how God showed His love among us:

He sent His one and only Son into the world

that we might live through Him.

All

This is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us

and sent His Son as an atoning sacrifice for our sins."

Minister

"For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith —

and this is not from yourselves,

it is the gift of God —

not by works, so that no one can boast.

All

For we are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus,

to do good works which God prepared in advance for us to do."

The congregation sings a psalm or hymn expressing the joy of the gospel.

Minister

"God, who said, 'Let light shine out of darkness,'

made His light shine in our hearts

to give us the light of the knowledge of the glory of God

in the face of Christ.

All

But we have this treasure in jars of clay

to show that this all-surpassing power is from God

and not from us.

Minister

We are hard pressed on every side, but not crushed;

perplexed, but not in despair;

persecuted, but not abandoned;

struck down, but not destroyed.

All

We always carry around in our body the death of Jesus,

so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body."

Minister

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation;

the old has gone, the new has come!

All

All this is from God, who reconciled us to Himself through Christ

and gave us the ministry of reconciliation."

Minister

"Therefore I urge you, brothers, in view of God's mercy,

offer your bodies as living sacrifices, holy and pleasing to God —

this is your spiritual act of worship.

Do not conform any longer to the pattern of this world,

but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.

Then you will be able to test and approve what God's will is —

His good, pleasing and perfect will...

Do not think of yourselves more highly than you ought,

but rather, think of yourselves with sober judgment,

in accordance with the measure of faith God has given you...

All

In Christ, we who are many form one body,

and each member belongs to all the others.

We have different gifts, according to the grace given us.

Minister

If a man's gift is prophesying, let him use it

in proportion to his faith.

If it is serving, let him serve;

if it is teaching, let him teach;

All

if it is encouraging, let him encourage;

if it is contributing to the needs of others,

let him give generously.

Minister

If it is leadership, let him govern diligently;

if it is showing mercy, let him do it cheerfully."

The congregation sings a psalm or hymn expressing joy in serving God, one another, and a world in need.

Then follows a prayer for which the following words may be used or in which the minister, using his own words, expresses the following thoughts:

Almighty God and loving Father in our Lord Jesus Christ,

how can we ever thank You enough for the joyful sound of the gospel in which You assure us that in Christ Jesus all our sins are forgiven and our life is renewed. We thank You for freely giving this marvellous gift of new life to us as Your church. We thank You that we share as Your people this costly treasure and may share it in turn with the needy world around us.

We thank You that in this congregation, too, Your love has made and is making us into one body with many members, and that You are giving us people endowed with Your gifts to help equip us for our witness to the gospel.

We thank You for the brothers who served among us, and we thank You for the families who supported them.

Lord, we need You for all we need in this church. What can we give others that You do not give us first? In all our doing and giving we fall so short of Your generosity! But we thank You that You do not leave us on our own: Your Son promised to be with us even to the end of the age — Your Spirit guides us into all truth.

Now You have given us these servants to serve as Your instruments, and we pray that Your Spirit will shape them for their work. Enable them to help us be bearers of Your good news in this day and in this place.

Here special points of intercession can be included.

Now open our hearts to Your Word that we may receive it in humility and joy and be doers of that Word in our daily lives.

Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

All

Amen.

The Ministry of the Word

Scripture reading(s).

Text and sermon.

A psalm or hymn is sung.

The Ordination / Installation

Minister

Congregation of Jesus Christ: Your Session has previously made known to you Names called to the office of elder and the name(s) of Names called to the office of deacon in this church. No one has brought forward anything lawful against their person or teaching. We shall therefore proceed, in the name of the Lord, to their ordination [and] installation.

Let us first call to mind what God's Word teaches concerning these offices within the church.

All members of the Body of Christ are office bearers, called to be prophets, priests and kings unto God, needing no mediators between God and man apart from Jesus Christ.

Christ, however, gathering His church on earth, gave her as His gifts special office bearers to equip and prepare God's people for the work of service.

Servants they are of God and of His people, following in the footsteps of Him who came not to be served but to serve, giving His life as a Ransom for many.

Servants they are of their risen Lord who said, "Whoever accepts anyone I send, accepts Me; and whoever accepts Me, accepts the One who sent Me."

All

"We are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation,

a people belonging to God,

that we may declare the praises of Him

who called us out of darkness into His wonderful light!"

When elders are ordained / installed:

Minister

Elders serve by governing the church in Christ's name.

They received this task when Christ entrusted the apostles and their successors with the keys of the kingdom of heaven. Thus elders are responsible for the spiritual well-being of God's people. They are to provide for true preaching and teaching, regular celebration of the sacraments, and faithful counsel and discipline.

Their call is also to promote fellowship among believers, hospitality and witness to all people, and good order in the church.

All

"For the grace of God that brings salvation has appeared to all men.

It teaches us to say 'no'

to ungodliness and worldly passions,

and to live self-controlled, upright and godly lives

in this present age,

while we wait for the blessed hope —

the glorious appearing of our great God and Saviour, Jesus Christ,

who gave Himself for us

to redeem us from all wickedness and to purify for Himself

a people that are His very own, eager to do what is good.

These then are the things you should teach.

Encourage and rebuke with all authority.

Do not let anyone despise you."

When deacons are ordained / installed:

Minister

Deacons serve by showing mercy to the church and to all people.

They received this task in the early church when the apostles designated special persons for the work of mercy. The deacons stimulate relief in Christ's name for victims of poverty, distress and injustice. Thereby they show that Christians live by the Spirit of the kingdom, fervently desiring to give life the shape of things to come.

In the household of God, deacons are therefore called to assess needs, promote stewardship, and develop programs of assistance.

When carried out with words of Christian encouragement, their work is done as unto the Lord Himself.

All

"Then the King will say...

'Come, you who are blessed by my Father;

take your inheritance, the kingdom prepared for you

since the creation of the world.

For I was hungry and you gave Me something to eat,

I was thirsty and you gave Me something to drink,

I was a stranger and you invited Me in,

I needed clothes and you clothed Me,

I was sick and you looked after Me,

I was in prison and you came to visit Me...

For whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of Mine, you did for Me.'"

Minister

These tasks of elders and deacons call for believers who are mature in the faith, whose lives are Christ-like, and who exercise their offices with diligent study of the Scriptures, with prayer, patience and humility.

Vows

Minister

Brothers Names, to express your acceptance of the office of Elder / Deacon, you are asked to stand, and here in the presence of God and His church to answer the following questions:

  1. Do you believe that, in the call of this congregation, you are being called by God Himself to serve in this holy office / these holy offices?
  1. Do you believe that the Scriptures of the Old and New Testament are the Word of God and that in them the way of salvation is taught completely, and do you accept the confessional standards of the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia as being in harmony with these Scriptures? Do you promise to defend this gospel and to reject all teachings that contradict it; to speak against all injustice and all untruth, and model for this congregation God's concern for compassion, justice and peace?
  1. Do you promise to carry out the work of your offices faithfully, to love the church and all its members, to live a life worthy of your calling and in submission to the government and discipline of the church?

Each office bearer answers individually:

By the grace of God, with all my heart, I do

Laying on of hands or raising the right hand in the gesture of blessing:

Minister

May God, our heavenly Father, who has called you to your holy office, guide you by His Word, equip you with His Holy Spirit, and so bless your ministry that His church may increase, His kingdom be extended, and His name be praised.

All

Amen.

Office bearer(s) remain standing, facing the congregation who also stand.

Minister

Congregation of our Lord Jesus Christ, to express your acceptance of this/these office bearers, you are asked to answer these questions:

  1. Do you, before God and one another, receive this servant / these servants as your elder(s) [and] deacon(s)?

Regarding elders

  1. Do you promise to receive the Word of truth from them in love and obedience as they seek to give you leadership and guidance in the work of the Lord?

Regarding deacons

  1. Do you promise to enable the diaconate to function meaningfully in its ministry of compassion and mercy?
  1. Do you promise to pray for these office bearers, respect and encourage them in their work, and faithfully work together with them to the honour and glory of Christ, the upbuilding of His church and the extension of His kingdom?

Congregation, in response to all these questions what is your answer?

All members of the local church answer:

All

By the grace of God, with all our heart, we do.

The congregation sings Psalm 134, and is then seated.

Charges

To Elders

Minister

I charge you, elders, to "take heed of yourselves and to all the flock,

in which the Holy Spirit has made you guardians, to feed the church of God which He obtained with the blood of His own Son."

Be a warm friend and Christ-like example to children. Give clear, cheerful and unpretentious guidance to the young people, who often wrestle with the overpowering spirits of this age. By word and example bear up God's people in their pain and weakness and celebrate their joys with them. Attentively and regularly encourage the aged with the hope of biblical promises.

Be (a) wise counsellor(s) who support and strengthen the ministers of the Word. Be compassionate, yet firm, and consistent in rebuke and discipline.

Master the Scriptures which alone can make God's people wise unto salvation. Pray continually for the church.

Remember at all times that, if you would truly be spiritual leaders in the household of faith, your entire lives must be mastered by your Lord.

To Deacons

I charge you, deacons, to inspire faithful stewardship in this congregation. Remind us that "From everyone who has been given much, much will be demanded; and from the one who has been entrusted with much, much more will be asked."

Weigh up the needs of causes and use the resources of this church with discernment.

Prompt us to seize new opportunities to worship God with offerings of wealth, time and ability. Realise that benevolence is a quality of our life in Christ, and not merely a matter of financial assistance. Therefore, be ministers of mercy to rich and poor alike, both within and outside the church. Teach us to be merciful.

Be discreetly compassionate to the needy. Encourage them with words that create hope in their hearts and with deeds that bring joy into their lives. Guide them to resources beyond your own.

Study the structures and patterns of modern society, so that you may be counsellors to their victims and prophetic critics of waste, injustice, and selfishness.

Let your lives be above reproach, lived as an example of Christ Jesus whose mind is being shaped within you.

To the Congregation, in all cases

I charge you, people of God, to receive your office bearers as Christ's gift to the church. Recognise in all of them your Lord's provision for healthy congregational life. Hold them in honour; take their counsel seriously; accept their help with thanks. Sustain them in prayer and encourage them with your support, especially when they feel the burdens of responsibility. Acknowledge them as the Lord's servants among you, and submit to them as you do to Christ.

Let us pray.

Our merciful Father in heaven, we thank You that You have provided faithful and gifted people to serve as elders and deacons.

As these new office bearers assume their responsibilities, fill them with Your Spirit, endow them with Your wisdom, and grant them strength from on high.

May they be faithful workers in Your vineyard. Under their guidance may Your church grow in every spiritual grace, in faith which is open and unashamed, and in the committed service that promotes Your reign in the world.

Deliver them from an attitude towards office that is commonplace or self-assured. In their work, grant them a sense of sustained awe which is rooted in daily adoration of You, their Lord. Through them may Your name be honoured and Your church be served.

Grant us, Your people, to accept them gladly, encourage them always and respect them for the sake of Your precious Son, our Lord, in whose name we pray.

All

Amen.

It may be appropriate to have the newly ordained/installed office bearer(s) sign the in the service. This Form should then first be read out for the information of the congregation, and may be found on page 185.

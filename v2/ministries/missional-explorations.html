Missional Explorations

Seed funding from the Church Planting Taskforce for local outreach that could one day grow into a church plant.

Talk to the CPTApplication form (PDF)

A stepping-stone to church planting

Missional Explorations are designed to identify local missional contexts that might one day become future church plants. They are a stepping-stone between a congregation's regular outreach and something that might take on a life of its own.

Under the supervision of the local church, a Missional Exploration might develop its own identity and, as a new congregation, lead to a formal church plant.

Why we fund them

The overall goal is to see the gospel proclaimed and the kingdom extended. Church planting is a big step, often seen as a “bridge too far”, and Missional Explorations stand in the gap between regular outreach and planting something new.

Whether or not a church plant results, we are confident that the gospel will be proclaimed, real communities will be reached and the Kingdom of God will be built up.

Funding

The Church Planting Taskforce (CPT) provides seed funding in partnership with local churches. It will match the local church's contribution up to $2,500.

  • Core-team training
  • Equipment
  • Venue hire
  • Printing promotional material

Who can apply

If you have found a missional opportunity that could grow into a church plant with the right training and financial support, please consider applying. Groups might meet around a sporting club, a playgroup, a school community or any other setting where Christians are involved.

The application asks for a description of the initiative and its leadership team, its plans, a detailed budget and who will report progress to the CPT. The CPT is happy to help churches with their application and may ask for more information before approving it.

Vision and values

The Christian Reformed Churches of Australia is committed to advancing the CRCA mission and vision in and through local churches. This mission and vision is lived out in submission to the Lord Jesus Christ and his command to make disciples of all nations.

Our Mission

We are a church reforming to reach the lost for Christ

Our Vision

We will multiply Bible-based churches which equip and nurture their members and grow throughout Australia and the world.

Our core values

Biblical values that shape the culture of a contemporary, Reformed, gospel-focused denomination.

Contemporary Reformed
We are confessional and contextual.
Eph. 4:3–6; 1 Cor. 11:2; 2 Thess. 2:15; 1 Cor. 9:19–23; Acts 14:8–18; 17:16–31
Word based
We are committed to biblical preaching, teaching, training, equipping.
Rom. 10:14–17; 2 Tim. 2:1–2; 3:16–17; Deut. 4:9–14; Col. 3:16; Eph. 4:11–16
Gospel focussed
We are on mission in word and deed, both locally and globally: kingdom-oriented, outward looking, evangelistic, disciple-making, community-hearted.
Micah 6:8; Isa. 1:17; 61:1–11; Luke 4:18–19; Matt. 5:13–16; 6:33; 25:31–46; 28:18–20; Phil. 1:14; Acts 1:6–8; 4:31; Col. 4:5–6; 1 Pet. 2:9–12; 3:15–16; Rev. 5:9–10
Local church empowering
We are light on structures and hierarchy, high on support, partnership, mutual accountability and encouragement.
Matt. 20:24–28; 25:14–30; Heb. 13:17; 1 Thess. 5:11; 2 Cor. 1:24; 8:23; Phil. 1:5; 4:15; 3 John 1:5–8; 1 Pet. 5:3
Prayerful urgency
We are humbly dependent upon God and filled with prayerful, sacrificial compassion for the lost in all the world.
Isa. 62:6; Acts 2:42; Matt. 6:5–13; Rom. 10:1; Eph. 6:10–20; 1 Tim. 2:1–4; 1 Thess. 5:17; Jude 20
Historic orthodoxy
We are complementarian, Scripture-regulated in worship, and unashamedly anchored in unchanging truth rather than cultural trends.
Gen. 1:27–28; 2:24; Deut. 12:32; Ps. 139:13–16; Matt. 19:4–6; Luke 24:27; Eph. 5:19–33; 1 Tim. 2:11–3:7; 2 Tim. 4:2; Heb. 12:28

How we go about it

Four tasks shape our work together: to pray for the Holy Spirit’s work among us, to grow healthy churches and plant new ones, to train disciples and leaders, and to align our life together with this mission.

In practice that means mentoring pastors, training people for evangelism and leadership, helping churches grow in health, planting new churches, and taking part in mission here and overseas.

These grow out of what we believe.

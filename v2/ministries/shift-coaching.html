Shift Coaching

A set of coaching questions to help CRCA pastors and church councils grow in love, discipleship, leadership and outreach.

Ask about Shift coachingDownload the Shift brochure

What Shift is

“I urge you to live a life worthy of the calling you have received.” (Ephesians 4:1)

Shift is all about movement – with the help of one another and the power of God to reach our broken places and move ever closer to living a life worthy of our callings as churches and leaders. We cannot lead others where we have not been ourselves.

All CRCA pastors are encouraged to set up a coaching relationship where a series of questions can be discussed regularly. The coach could be a ministerial peer, an elder, a wise and mature church member, or a professional coach. Church councils are also asked to review what they are doing for the professional development of their pastors.

Questions for coaching conversations

“As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another.” (Proverbs 27:17)

The questions cover four areas: loving community, discipleship, multiplying leaders and reaching the lost. Answer each on a scale of 1 to 10, where 10 is best, then ask what would move you one step closer to 10.

  • Love one another: How would you rate your own passion and love for God and His people? How visible to those outside the church is the love your members have for one another?
  • Discipleship: What is your own “discipleship temperature”? What are you personally doing to grow disciples in your church? How would you rate the culture of “growing disciples” among your members, not just from the pastor?
  • Multiplying leadership: How would you as pastor rate yourself in training and multiplying leaders? How are your existing ministry leaders going at raising up leaders, and what most holds your church back from this?
  • Reaching the lost: How would you rate your own “outreach temperature”, and that of your church? Where could your church use its resources, ministries, time, care, energy and love for people in the community who are still far from God?

Professional development

These questions can be asked regularly at church council or session meetings, at Classis meetings, or during church visitation.

  • What is your church council or session doing to make sure your minister has a plan for ongoing personal and professional learning, and is making progress on it? What could you provide to help, such as time, money or coaching?
  • As a minister, what are you doing for your personal and professional development? What would help you carry out your professional development plan? What has helped you grow most that might also help your colleagues in Classis?

Suggested reading

The Shift questions come with suggested books for each area. These include:

  • 5 Things to Pray for Your Church by Rachel Jones
  • 6 Steps Toward Loving Your Church by Colin Marshall and Tony Payne
  • The 10-Second Rule by Clare de Graaf
  • The Trellis and the Vine and The Vine Project by Colin Marshall and Tony Payne
  • Jesus Christ Disciplemaker by Bill Hull
  • Discipleship Essentials by Greg Ogden
  • Just For Starters by Phillip Jensen and Tony Payne
  • Short Steps for Long Gains by Simon Manchester
  • Christian Living For Starters by Gordon Cheng
  • Leadership Essentials by Greg Ogden and Daniel Meyer
  • Passing the Baton by Colin Marshall
  • Wisdom in Leadership by Craig Hamilton
  • Organic Outreach for Ordinary People and Organic Outreach for Churches by Kevin Harney
  • Me, an Evangelist? by William J McKay
  • Witness Essentials by Daniel Meyer
  • The Andy Stanley Leadership Podcast

Getting help

The TRAIN Workgroup can offer encouragement, training and resources for setting up a good coaching relationship. Contact the Synodical Administrator to find out more.

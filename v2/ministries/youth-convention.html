Convention 2027 is sold out

Convention 2027 is full. You can still register to be added to the waitlist.

Registration opened on 1 March 2026 and closes on 30 November 2026.

What Convention is

Convention exists to bring together the young adults of the CRCA for a week in God's Word, worship and community. With a focus on personal discipleship, it serves as a "spiritual reset" for the year ahead.

Convention seeks to support the local church in raising up young people who will be lifelong, disciple-making disciples of Jesus.

Convention 2027 will include plenty of time opening God's Word, worshipping together, hanging out at the village, making memories with like-minded people and some fun activities. It is a week to grow in your faith with friends new and old.

The 2027 theme: Together in Christ

The theme for Convention 2027 is "Together in Christ: grasping God's glory in Jesus and his Church".

One of the great joys of Convention is meeting up with many other disciples and seekers to discover Jesus, praise Jesus and grow in Jesus. Together, we are an expression of the church, the body and the bride of Christ.

Through the book of Ephesians, Convention 2027 will look at the deep, rich, mysterious and glorious union of Christ and his Church. God's big reveal in the Gospel is Jesus and his resurrected Body. The church is not just a human organisation, but a God-given organism, powered by his Spirit.

This shapes every part of our lives: our identity, our purpose, our relationships and our future, both individually and together. Those who are "in Christ" are both recipients and channels of God's outpouring of grace.

Join us as we consider the joy and the calling of union with Jesus, as his Body and Bride together.

Keynote speaker: Murray Capill

Murray Capill works at Reformed Theological College in Melbourne, where he loves helping to train and equip the next generation of pastors and gospel workers. He also regularly speaks at camps and conferences, teaching the Bible and helping equip God's people for ministry.

Murray grew up in New Zealand. He is married to Wendy, and they have five adult children and quite a few grandchildren. After hours you would probably find him enjoying the outdoors, doing jobs around home, having a coffee or hanging out with the grandkids.

Dates and place

Convention runs from the afternoon of Monday 11 January to the morning of Saturday 16 January 2027.

It is held at Busselton Baptist Camping Centre in Busselton, Western Australia.

A free bus leaves Perth Airport at 2pm sharp on 11 January and returns to Perth Airport at 4pm on 16 January. If you need a seat on the bus, select it in your registration.

Who can come

Convention is for anyone aged 16 to 28.

To support the spiritual growth of our young adults, the Convention Planning Committee has agreed to a strict age policy. If you have questions about the age policy, email admin@crcaconvention.au.

Cost and what is included

The cost covers five nights' accommodation at Busselton Baptist Camping Centre and all meals during Convention. It also goes towards the guest speakers and panellists who present during the week, and some fun activities. More details will be shared closer to the time.

If the cost is a barrier, talk to your church or youth group. The fundraising ideas sheet below has suggestions for raising money together.

  • Early bird: $549 (closed 30 June 2026)
  • Full price: $599 (registrations close 30 November 2026)

What a week at Convention looks like

This is the timetable from Remain, the January 2023 Convention, to give you a feel for the week. The 2027 program may differ.

Most days started with a leaders' meeting and breakfast, then a morning rally with Bible teaching, time for individual reflection and small groups before lunch. Afternoons held activity electives, growth electives, team games ("Tribe Wars") or an outing to the beach. Evenings had dinner, a "Dwell" worship and teaching session and time at the village. There was also a DJ and disco party, a testimony night and a closing session before departure.

Recordings and reflection questions from Remain 2023

If you want a sneak peek at what Convention is like, you can watch the four main talks from Remain, the January 2023 Convention, below. Mike Raiter and Craig Verdouw spoke from John 13 to 17 on what it means to remain in Jesus.

After Convention, the Convention Planning Committee and Phillip Scheepers from Reformed Theological College wrote reflection questions for church groups to use with their Convention crew. Each day has a short summary, Bible passages and discussion questions, and the guide ends with a prayer.

Day 1, "In Jesus, in Life" (Mike Raiter; John 15:1-8 and Isaiah 5:1-7): our identity is not found in our location, background or status, but in belonging to Jesus. He calls us to remain in him and bear fruit.

Day 2, "In Jesus, in the Church" (Craig Verdouw; John 13:1-17, 15:12-17 and 17:20-23): instead of reaching for the top, Jesus went low and washed his disciples' feet. Sacrificial service builds up the church and witnesses to the gospel.

Day 3, "In Jesus, in the Spirit" (Mike Raiter; John 16:4-11): Jesus said his going away would be good for his disciples, because it would bring the Holy Spirit, who points us to Christ.

Day 4, "In Jesus, in the World" (Craig Verdouw; John 15:18-25 and 17:13-24): Jesus sends his followers into a world that can be hostile, but he protects them through the Word, the Spirit and the faith they have been given, as they stick together for the sake of the gospel.

Form for the Baptism of Adults

a baptismal service

____________________________________________________

NOTE:

Sessions can combine a Form for the Baptism of the Children of Believers with the . If it is an adult baptism that does not include children, the segment of the Form for Baptism dealing with children being in the covenant can be omitted.

In this Form:

(p) before a paragraph means:

This is to be said where public profession of faith is also made by baptised members.

(c) before a paragraph means:

This is to be said where both adults and their children receive baptism.

It is meaningful and Scriptural, when there are such children, that they receive the sacrament in the same service as their parents.

____________________________________________________

Call to Worship and Opening of Service

Minister

Shout for joy to the LORD, all the earth,

serve the Lord with gladness;

come before Him with joyful songs.

Know that the LORD is God!

All

It is He who made us, and we are His;

we are His people, the sheep of His pasture.

Minister

Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise;

give thanks to Him and praise His name.

All

For the LORD is good and His love endures forever;

His faithfulness continues through all generations.

Minister

With joy, you will draw water from the wells of salvation!

Optional: silent prayer.

Minister

Our help is in the name of the Lord

All

Who made heaven and earth.

Minister

Who faithfully keeps His word

All

and never forsakes the works of His hands.

Minister

Grace to you and peace from God our Father

and from our Lord Jesus Christ,

in the fellowship of the Holy Spirit.

All

Amen.

A psalm or hymn of praise is sung.

Minister

Our risen Lord Jesus said to His followers: "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me. Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptising them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely, I will be with you always, to the very end of the age." By giving us this sign of being washed with water our Lord tells us that we are unclean before God. Our sins need to be taken away if we are to enter the kingdom of God.

Let us come before the Lord in prayer and humbly confess our sins.

All

Almighty and most merciful Father,

we have strayed from Your ways like lost sheep.

We have left undone what we ought to have done,

and we have done what we ought not to have done.

We have followed our own ways and the desires of our own hearts.

We have broken Your holy laws in our thoughts, words and deeds.

Through our sins we have deserved Your condemnation.

O Lord God, Lamb of God, Son of the Father,

Who takes away the sin of the world,

have mercy on us and hear our prayer. Amen.

Minister

People of God, in our baptism we are assured that the blood of Jesus Christ, God's Son, cleanses us of all sin, and that His Spirit renews us from day to day. We and our children are called back to the triune God in a restored covenant relationship. For when we are baptised into the name of the Father, God the Father assures us that He adopts us as His children and surrounds us with His love and care even in times of testing and trial.

All

God, our Father and Creator,

we praise and thank You that You gave us life

and adopted us as Your children.

Minister

When we are baptised into the name of the Son, God the Son assures us that through His death and resurrection we are set free from the condemnation of sin, and are made right with God by faith in Him alone.

All

Son of God, Saviour, we praise and thank You

for Your sacrifice and victory!

Minister

When we are baptised into the name of the Holy Spirit, God the Holy Spirit assures us that He, the Giver of faith and new life, will live within us and change us by His power from day to day until at last we shall be presented spotless before God in the full splendour of life eternal.

All

Holy Spirit, our Guide and Companion,

we praise and thank You for the life and hope You give us!

Minister

So God, who promises us all these things, calls us in this sacrament to begin living that new life in purity and holiness. He made a covenant with us: "I will be your God, you will be My people."

He calls us to love Him with all our heart, soul, mind and strength. He calls us to be on His side in the battle against Satan and his works, both within ourselves and in the world around us. Jesus calls us to carry our cross in His footsteps and accept suffering as living members of His body.

If then through weakness we fall into sin, it is He who calls us never to doubt His mercy nor to remain in sin or give ourselves up to despair. We are to remember joyfully that God's covenant with us is for ever, and so with His help we can overcome and are encouraged to go on in His service.

All

Sing Psalm 79a:2 or similar psalm or hymn.

O LORD, by grace we are Your chosen nation;

we are Your sheep, Your new-born generation,

You are our Shepherd, and will ever lead us

and in the bounty of Your pastures feed us.

Then father tells to son

the deeds that You have done:

Your great redemption's story!

So sounds Your praise along

in one unbroken song

to Your unending glory!

The Service of the Word

After the sermon a psalm or hymn is sung.

Then an elder shall say:

Brothers and sisters in Christ,

It gives me great joy, on behalf of Session, to present to you Name(s) of Candidate(s) for Adult Baptism, who has expressed the desire to join this Christian church and has been instructed in the teachings of our faith. He has professed his faith before Session and now wants to do so publicly before this congregation and so receive the sacrament of baptism

(c) with his children Name(s) of Child(ren).

(p) We also gladly present to you the following baptised members who, having been nurtured in the Christian faith, now seeks full communicant membership in this church: Name(s) of Baptised Member(s). He, too, has professed his faith before Session and desires to do so before you all.

We thank our Lord who in His grace has called him and brought him to this turning point in his life, trusting that He who began a good work in him will carry it on to completion until the Day of Christ Jesus.

Profession

It is suggested that, as the minister calls each individual candidate by name, (s)he arises and faces the congregation.

Name of Candidate, the Lord Jesus says: "You did not choose Me, but I chose you to go and bear fruit — fruit that will last." "Whoever acknowledges Me before men, I will also acknowledge him before My Father in heaven."

It is God in His sovereign love who chose you,

(p) who keeps His covenant of grace with you,

who gives faith and unites you with Jesus Christ, and calls you into the full fellowship of His church. In the presence of God and His people, we now invite you to profess your faith in Him.

At this point one of two procedures can be followed:

EITHER:

each candidate says the following. Candidates may be encouraged to use their own words but the following areas are to be covered substantially:
OR:

alternatively, if there are too many people for each to speak or it is deemed inappropriate for each to make a full verbal confession, the minister may address the following questions:
1. I profess that Jesus Christ is my Lord and Saviour, and I want to follow and serve Him as His disciple. I believe He is the Son of God sent to redeem the world. I love and trust Him as the One who saves me from sin; I continue to turn from my rebellion against my Lord, and embrace Him as Lord of my life with repentance and joy.1. Do you believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God sent to redeem the world; do you love and trust Him as the One who saves you from your sin; will you continue to turn from your rebellion against your Lord, and do you with repentance and joy embrace Him as the Lord of your life?
2. I acknowledge that the teaching of the Old and New Testaments, summarised in and our Confessions and taught in this Christian church, is the true and complete doctrine of salvation.2. Do you acknowledge that the teaching of the Old and New Testaments, summarised in and our Confessions and taught in this Christian church, is the true and complete doctrine of salvation?
(p) 3. I accept the gracious promises of God sealed to me in my baptism and affirm my union with Christ and His church which baptism signifies.(p) 3. And, baptised members, do you accept the gracious promises of God sealed to you in your baptism and do you affirm your union with Christ and His church which baptism signifies?
4. I promise to do all I can, with the help of the Holy Spirit, to strengthen my love and commitment to Christ by sharing faithfully in the life of the church and its means of grace, honouring and submitting to its supervision and discipline, and I join with the people of God in doing the work of the Lord wherever I am.4. Do you promise to do all you can, with the help of the Holy Spirit, to strengthen your love and commitment to Christ by sharing faithfully in the life of the church and its means of grace, honouring and submitting to its supervision and discipline, and do you join with the people of God in doing the work of the Lord wherever you are?

Name(s) of Candidate(s), what is your answer to these questions?

Each replies in turn:

I profess that Jesus Christ is my Lord and Saviour and I want to follow and serve Him as His disciple. So, with all my heart, by the grace of God, I say "Yes!" to these questions.

The minister addresses each person individually after his/her profession is made by way of:

  • an individual text,
  • a word of promise, exhortation or doxology,
  • and, if appropriate, an individual personal word.

At this point a psalm or hymn may be sung.

Then all are requested to be seated.

Minister

(c) God graciously includes our children in His covenant, and all His promises are for them as well as for us. Jesus Himself took little children in His arms and blessed them, and the apostle Paul said that children of believers are holy. So, just as the children of the Old Covenant received the sign of circumcision, our children are given, with us, the sign of baptism: "you and your house-hold!" We are, therefore, always to teach our young ones that they have been set apart by baptism as God's own children.

In all cases, the minister continues:

Let us pray.

We praise You, Almighty Father, for saving the family of faith carried in the ark when the waters of the flood covered a rebellious world. We praise You, for setting Your people free as You led them safely through the waters of the Red Sea that swallowed up the oppressor.

All

Father, You are our God, we are Your people.

Minister

We praise You, Christ our Lord, for going down into the waters of Jordan and coming up to receive the Spirit; for sinking into the depth of our eternal death, to be raised up Lord of our life!

All

Christ Jesus, Son of God, You are our Shepherd,

we are the sheep of Your pasture!

Minister

We praise You, Holy Spirit, for dwelling in us. We pray that You will separate us from sin and openly mark us with a faith that can stand the light of day and endure the dark of night.

All

Spirit of God, keep prompting us to glad obedience.

Minister

We pray now for Names of all to be baptised, including children (if any), that he, daily dying to sin and raised to the new life You give, may carry his cross joyfully in the Master's footsteps, till on the great Day of Jesus he, too, will see His face in never-ending delight.

All

Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, You are our God, we are Your people. Amen.

Minister, addressing adult(s) to be baptised:

Do you now wish to be baptised in the name of the triune God, and will you receive your baptism as a sign and seal that God accepts you in Christ, forgives all your sins, and incorporates you into His church?

Each confessor:

I do, with all my heart.

The Minister continues:

(c) Do you furthermore believe that your child(ren) Name(s) of Child(ren), though sinful by nature right from the beginning of his life and consequently deserving of God's judgment and even eternal death is, by God's mercy, holy in Christ and therefore, as a member of Christ's church, ought to be baptised?

Finally, do you promise, in reliance on the Holy Spirit and with the help of the Christian community, to do all in your power to instruct Name(s) of Child(ren) in the Christian faith, cause him to be so instructed, and to lead him by your example into a life of Christian discipleship?

Parents:

By the grace of God, we do.

When both adults and child(ren) are to be baptised, the adults receive the sacrament first. They shall kneel and receive the baptism on their foreheads.

They then shall arise and present their child(ren) for baptism.

Minister

, I baptise you into the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.Full Name

Congregation, please arise.

Do you, the people of the Lord, promise to receive Names of those baptised, both young and old in love into your fellowship as members of the body of Christ, and do you promise to encourage them in doing the work of the Lord?

All

By the grace of God, we do.

Minister

I now ask all those baptised members who have just professed their faith to join those who have just been baptised.

Raising his hands over the confessor(s) or laying his hand(s) upon his/her head:

The God of all grace, who has called you to His eternal glory in Christ, will Himself restore you and make you strong, firm and steadfast. To Him be the power for ever and ever!

THE LORD BLESS YOU AND KEEP YOU.

THE LORD MAKE HIS FACE TO SHINE UPON YOU

AND BE GRACIOUS TO YOU;

THE LORD TURN HIS FACE TOWARD YOU

AND GIVE YOU PEACE.

Amen.

An elder then addresses the confessor(s):

In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ I now welcome you all to full communicant participation in the life of the church. I welcome you to its responsibilities, its privileges, its sufferings and its joys.

All

Thanks be to God! We rejoice in your profession and promise you our love, encouragement and prayers.

A psalm or hymn is sung.

Then there could be a time of symbolising the welcome, e.g., wishing each other God's peace, shaking hands, giving of gifts or allocation of tasks.

It would be very meaningful if, in the same service or in another service on the same day, the Lord's Supper is celebrated.

Minister

Let us give thanks to the Lord.

Our Father in heaven, we thank You for Jesus Christ, for the new life given in Him, and for the one faith, one hope, and one baptism which your people have shared through the ages.

We rejoice that Name of Adults Baptised is now one with Your church and that we have received him as a members of this congregation.

(p) We praise You that Baptised members who have professed their faith have come to see the riches You promised them when they were children, and have responded in faith and joy to the call of their Saviour and Lord.

(c) Enable Name of Children who have just received the sacrament of baptism to stand up, in Your good time, among Your people and openly acknowledge Your great goodness and grace in Jesus Christ. To that end, Lord, give wisdom to their parents and all of us to guide them in Your ways.

Encourage us all to fight the works of Satan and give us endurance in trial and hardship. Then, when the great Day of Jesus comes, we will meet Him with joy and celebrate with all Your people the feast of Your eternal presence and glory.

Through Jesus Christ our Lord, Who taught us to pray:

All

Our Father in heaven,

hallowed be Your name,

Your Kingdom come,

Your will be done

on earth as in heaven.

Give us today our daily bread.

Forgive us our sins,

as we forgive those

who sin against us.

Lead us not into temptation

but deliver us from the evil one.

For the kingdom, the power and the glory

are Yours,

now and for ever.

Amen.
